# VALORANT Archipelago 0.6.4

Released October 7, 2026

## Changed

- **Weapons count from the next round that starts after you get them**, whether another player sent them or you
  rented them. Get one between rounds and it counts for the round about to start; get one mid-round and it counts
  from the round after. Kills made with it before never count.
  - **Received weapons** used to count for the whole match they arrived in, including earlier kills. The client goes
    by when it saw the item arrive, so a weapon received while the client was closed counts from the next time you
    connect. Weapons you already have keep counting as before.
  - **Rentals** bought between rounds now cover the rest of the match you're in, instead of waiting for your next
    match; the rental is used up by that match. Renting in the menus or in agent select still covers your whole next
    match. With Strict enforcement, having used the weapon before renting it still voids the match.
  - The client says so when a weapon arrives mid-match ("count from the next round that starts").

## Fixed

- A weapon rented between rounds could count for kills made earlier in that same match if the client noticed the
  match again mid-match (for example after restarting the client).

## Updating

Reinstall `valorant.apworld`. No need to regenerate.
