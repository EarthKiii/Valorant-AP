# VALORANT Archipelago 0.2.2

Released October 5, 2026

## New

- **The client shows your Radianite count** next to the Connect button ("Radianite: 5 / 12", collected / needed).

## Bridge

- **Progress window:** the bridge now opens a small window with:
  - your Radianite count (green once you have enough), your slot name, and status dots for the client, VALORANT and
    live stats;
  - a banner for the match you're in, saying whether it counts or why not;
  - every agent's progress, with a bar per stat toward its next tier.
- It updates as soon as something changes, including live kills.
- `SHOW_WINDOW=false` in `bridge/.env` keeps the bridge headless. Closing the window stops the bridge.

## Updating

Reinstall `valorant.apworld`, restart the client, and run `npm start` for the bridge. No need to regenerate.
