# VALORANT Archipelago 0.2.3

Released October 5, 2026

## Bridge

The progress window is redesigned:

- **Agent card:** the selected agent's portrait, role and checks count, with a bar and tier dots for each stat. It
  switches to the agent you're playing whenever a match starts, and you can click any agent to see theirs. Locked
  agents are grayed, with a hint to find them in the multiworld.
- **Agent grid:** every agent in your world with their in-game icon, grayed with a lock until you unlock them.
- **Weapons:** grouped like the buy menu (Sidearms, SMGs, Shotguns, Rifles, Snipers, Heavies), grayed with a lock
  until unlocked.
- Icons come from valorant-api.com and are cached after the first load.

## Updating

Reinstall `valorant.apworld`, restart the client, and run `npm start` for the bridge. No need to regenerate.
